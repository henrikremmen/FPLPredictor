# Model refit and news refresh: 10 October 2026

Completed at 01:01 Europe/Oslo, ahead of the GW6 deadline at 12:00.

The approved `market_hgb+market_fallback+minutes_mixture_15+quantile_hgb`
model was refitted on 163,072 historical rows from 2020/21–2025/26 using
the existing production refit function. Model selection and hyperparameters
were unchanged. Existing promotion results were retained; this run did not
repeat model selection or establish an improvement in accuracy.

New model: `artifacts/models/production_market_0erysib7/model.joblib`.
Its settings record the previous model and original evaluation artifact.

Official FPL player news, availability, prices, fixtures and set-piece context
were downloaded, together with configured odds and cross-competition schedules.
The forecasts use the new model. News text is captured for display and audit;
official playing-chance flags adjust app scores. Articles are not training labels.

was also checked for the current round and deadline.
