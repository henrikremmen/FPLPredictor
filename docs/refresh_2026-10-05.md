## Limitations and follow-up

The app applies today's playing chance throughout the multiweek horizon. This can over-penalize later rounds for short injuries. Improving this requires timestamped, confirmed return estimates and validation.

The fixture source currently calculates club workload. It does not map individual international minutes or travel. Zero club matches during the break do not establish that a player rested.

Refresh after the final internationals on 6 October, after press conferences on 9 October, and before the 12:00 deadline on 10 October. These are suggested checks, not a scheduled background job. Reload the team in an open app to replace predictions cached in an existing session.

